GAIA, an AI agent for managing a fleet of payment terminals

Where
Cielo, Latin America's largest payment acquirer
When
2026, live in Microsoft Teams
Role
Designed and built it, from the data layer to the agent
Stack
Databricks and Databricks Genie, GPT-5.6, Claude Opus 4.8, Microsoft Teams

The problem. A fleet of POS terminals changes state every day: installed, moved, recovered, lost, in stock. Knowing where the fleet stood meant knowing which report to open and whom to ask, so most people did neither.

What I built. GAIA, an agent that lives in Microsoft Teams and answers questions about the fleet in plain language, grounded in the operation's data integrated in Databricks. It covers machine status, the rules that define the active and inactive park, losses and the gap in the park, key accounts, asset recovery, movements, position and inventory. GPT-5.6 and Claude Opus 4.8 do the reasoning; Databricks Genie turns the questions into queries.

What it returned. One place to ask about the fleet, open company-wide to every employee with access, instead of a chain of reports and messages.

Role

When

Role

Stack

The problem. A brokerage runs on leads, and most of the time between first contact and a quote goes to the same questions, asked on WhatsApp at all hours. I own the brokerage, so I had the data and the pain.

What I built. Claudia, a WhatsApp agent that answers, qualifies and routes leads; the n8n orchestration behind her; LLM classifiers for intent and lead quality; voice replies through ElevenLabs; and the CRM she writes to. The stack is self-hosted so the business keeps its data and its margins.

A shopping assistant that lives inside WhatsApp

Where
HUNTER, my own product
When
2025 to today, in pilot
Role
Founder and engineer
Stack
Node.js, Claude API, Supabase, Railway, Z-API

The problem. In Brazil, people research purchases in chat, not in apps. An assistant has to work inside a WhatsApp conversation, with all the ambiguity that brings.

What I built. A multi-agent pipeline that reads a request, detects what the person actually wants, searches and compares options, and answers in the same conversation. Supabase holds state, Railway runs it.

What it returned. Currently in pilot.
